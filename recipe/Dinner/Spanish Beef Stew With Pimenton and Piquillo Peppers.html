Spanish Beef Stew With Pimenton and Piquillo Peppers

.cook
#beef stew #Spanish Beef Stew #beef stew recipe

This Spanish beef stew combines tender braised beef with smoky paprika, roasted peppers, and red wine for a hearty, deeply flavored dish.

👥 6 servings 🍽️ Dinner, Entree ⏱️ Prep Time: 5 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 4 hours 40 minutes 🌍 Spanish 👤 Jennifer Olvera 📖 www.seriouseats.com

🥘 Ingredients

  • Worcestershire sauce
    1 tbsp
  • bay leaves
    2
  • beef stew meat
    2 lbs
  • button mushrooms (quartered if large)
    8 oz
  • carrot (peeled and cut into large chunks)
    1
  • diced tomatoes
    1 28oz can
  • dry red wine
    1 cup
  • extra-virgin olive oil
    2 tbsp
  • garlic
    3 cloves
  • ground black pepper
  • kosher salt
  • piquillo peppers (drained)
    1
  • smoked Spanish paprika
    1.5 tsp
  • sour cream
    1 cup
  • thyme
    4 sprigs
  • tomato paste
    2.5 tbsp
  • yellow onion (finely chopped)
    1

🍳 Cookware

  • Dutch oven
  1. 1
    Adjust oven rack to lower-middle position and preheat oven to 275°F (135°C).
  2. 2
    Pat beef stew meat dry with paper towels and sprinkle all over with kosher salt and ground black pepper .
    beef stew meat: 2 lbs, kosher salt, ground black pepper
  3. 3
    In a large Dutch oven , heat extra-virgin olive oil over high heat until shimmering. Cook half of the beef until browned on all sides, about ⏱️ 4-5 minutes , adjusting heat as necessary to avoid burning. Transfer browned beef to a plate and repeat with remaining beef.
    extra-virgin olive oil: 2 tbsp
  4. 4
    Reduce heat to medium. Add yellow onion and garlic and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and lightly browned, about ⏱️ 7 minutes .
    yellow onion: 1 (finely chopped), garlic: 3 cloves
  5. 5
    Add piquillo peppers , carrot , button mushrooms , smoked Spanish paprika , dry red wine , diced tomatoes , tomato paste , Worcestershire sauce , bay leaves , and thyme . Bring to a simmer over medium-high heat. Add beef and juices from the plate to the pot. Cover and transfer to the oven. Cook until beef is tender, about ⏱️ 4 hours .
    piquillo peppers: 1 (drained), carrot: 1 (peeled and cut into large chunks), button mushrooms: 8 oz (quartered if large), smoked Spanish paprika: 1.5 tsp, dry red wine: 1 cup, diced tomatoes: 1 28oz can, tomato paste: 2.5 tbsp, Worcestershire sauce: 1 tbsp, bay leaves: 2, thyme: 4 sprigs
  6. 6
    Remove stew from oven and discard thyme and bay leaves. Reduce over medium-low heat until sauce is thick enough to coat the back of a spoon, about ⏱️ 5 minutes . Season to taste with kosher salt and ground black pepper .
    kosher salt, ground black pepper
  7. 7
    Let cool slightly. Serve with sour cream or plain yogurt (or 1 cup plain yogurt).
    sour cream: 1 cup
