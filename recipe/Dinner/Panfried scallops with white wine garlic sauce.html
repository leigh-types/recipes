Panfried scallops with white wine garlic sauce

.cook
#How to cook scallops #Panfried scallops #Scallop recipes

Panfried scallops with white wine garlic sauce is an easy and delicious recipe, perfect for a date night in. Served with risotto, it's a showstopper.

👥 4 servings 🍽️ Dinner ⏱️ Prep Time: 10 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 10 minutes 🌍 American 👤 Alida Ryder 📖 simply-delicious-food.com

🥘 Ingredients

  • butter
    2 tbsp
  • garlic (minced)
    3-4 cloves
  • lemon juice
    1 tsp
  • olive oil
    1 tbsp
  • parsley (finely chopped)
    2 tbsp
  • pepper
    1/2 tsp
  • salt
    1 tsp
  • scallops
    500 g
  • white wine ((I used Chardonnay)
    1/2 cup

🍳 Cookware

  • frying pan
  1. 1
    Heat a large, non-stick frying pan over high heat.
  2. 2
    Pat scallops dry with paper towels. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper .
    scallops: 500 g, olive oil: 1 tbsp, salt: 1 tsp, pepper: 1/2 tsp
  3. 3
    Add the scallops to the hot pan (do this in batches to avoid overcrowding the pan) and allow to panfry for ⏱️ 2-3 minutes per side until they are golden brown and seared on both sides.
  4. 4
    Remove from the pan and set aside.
  5. 5
    Melt butter in the pan then add garlic . Allow to cook for ⏱️ 30 seconds then pour in white wine ), lemon juice and parsley .
    butter: 2 tbsp, garlic: 3-4 cloves (minced), white wine: 1/2 cup ((I used Chardonnay), lemon juice: 1 tsp, parsley: 2 tbsp (finely chopped)
  6. 6
    Bring the sauce to a simmer then cook for ⏱️ 3-5 minutes until the sauce has reduced slightly.
  7. 7
    Add the scallops back into the pan and allow to heat through.
  8. 8
    Serve with sides of your choice, such as lemon risotto and crusty bread.
