Greek Chicken and Orzo Recipe

#Greek Chicken and Orzo

This easy Greek Chicken and Orzo recipe comes together in one pot, making it the perfect mid-week meal! Fresh, simple & incredibly delicious.

👥 4 servings 🍽️ Main Course ⏱️ Prep Time: 15 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 30 minutes 🌍 Greek, Mediterranean 📖 www.budgetbytes.com

🥘 Ingredients

  • black pepper
    1/2 tsp
  • butter
    1 tbsp
  • chicken broth
    2 cups
  • chicken thighs (boneless, skinless, about 1.3 lbs.)
    4
  • garlic (minced)
    2 cloves
  • garlic powder
    1/2 tsp
  • grape tomatoes (halved)
    1/2 pint
  • kalamata olives (pitted)
    1/3 cup
  • lemon juice
    2 tbsp
  • olive oil
    1 tbsp
  • onion (diced)
    1
  • oregano
    1.5 tsp
  • orzo
    1.25 cups
  • paprika
    1 tsp
  • parsley (chopped)
    2 tbsp
  • red bell pepper (diced)
    1
  • salt
    1 tsp
  • thyme
    1/2 tsp

🍳 Cookware

  • large deep skillet
  1. 1
    In a small bowl, combine paprika , thyme , garlic powder , oregano , salt , and black pepper .
    paprika: 1 tsp, thyme: 1/2 tsp, garlic powder: 1/2 tsp, oregano: 1 tsp, salt: 3/4 tsp, black pepper: 1/4 tsp
  2. 2
    Blot chicken thighs dry with a paper towel, then season both sides liberally with the seasoning blend.
    chicken thighs: 4 (boneless, skinless, about 1.3 lbs.)
  3. 3
    Heat olive oil and butter in a large deep skillet over medium heat. Once the skillet is hot, add the chicken and cook on each side until golden brown and cooked through ( ⏱️ 5 minutes per side). Remove the chicken from the skillet and transfer to a clean plate. Cover with aluminum foil to keep warm.
    olive oil: 1 tbsp, butter: 1 tbsp
  4. 4
    In the same skillet, add onion , red bell pepper , and garlic . Sauté for ⏱️ 3-4 minutes until onions are translucent.
    onion: 1 (diced), red bell pepper: 1 (diced), garlic: 2 cloves (minced)
  5. 5
    Now add in orzo uncooked, oregano , salt , black pepper , and chicken broth . Stir to combine and scrape up any browned bits that may be stuck at the bottom of the pan.
    orzo: 1.25 cups, oregano: 1/2 tsp, salt: 1/4 tsp, black pepper: 1/4 tsp, chicken broth: 2 cups
  6. 6
    Return the chicken to the pan and nestle it down into the orzo. Place a lid on the skillet, turn the heat up to medium-high, and let the broth come to a full boil. Once the broth is boiling, turn the heat down to medium-low and let the skillet simmer for about ⏱️ 12 minutes or until most of the broth is absorbed.
  7. 7
    After about ⏱️ 12 minutes , add in grape tomatoes and let the skillet continue to simmer, without the lid, for ⏱️ 3-4 minutes more.
    grape tomatoes: 1/2 pint (halved)
  8. 8
    Remove the skillet from the heat and finish by stirring in lemon juice , kalamata olives , and topping with parsley . Enjoy!
    lemon juice: 2 tbsp, kalamata olives: 1/3 cup (pitted), parsley: 2 tbsp (chopped)
