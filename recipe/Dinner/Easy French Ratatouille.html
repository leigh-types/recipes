Easy French Ratatouille

.cook

Make this thick and silky French stew of eggplant, zucchini, sweet peppers, and ripe summer tomatoes before summer ends.

👥 8 servings 🍽️ Dinner, Main dish ⏱️ Prep Time: 20 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 2 hours 15 minutes 🌍 Europe, French 👤 Laure Joliet 📖 www.thekitchn.com

🥘 Ingredients

  • basil leaves (thinly sliced)
    1/4 cup
  • bay leaf
    1
  • bell pepper (dice)
    1 large
  • eggplant (large dice)
    1.5 lbs
  • fresh thyme
    2 sprigs
  • freshly ground black pepper
  • garlic (minced)
    2 cloves
  • kosher salt
  • olive oil
    5 tbsp
  • tomatoes (large dice)
    1 lbs
  • yellow onion (diced)
    1
  • zucchini or summer squash (large dice)
    1.5 lbs

🍳 Cookware

  • Dutch oven
  1. 1
    Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven or heavy-bottomed pot over medium-high heat until shimmering.
    olive oil: 2 tbsp
  2. 2
    Add eggplant , season generously with kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper , and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned in spots, about 2 minutes. Transfer to a large bowl.
    eggplant: 1.5 lbs (large dice), kosher salt, freshly ground black pepper
  3. 3
    Add olive oil to the pot. Add zucchini or summer squash , season generously with kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper , and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned in spots, about 2 minutes. Transfer to the bowl with the eggplant.
    olive oil: 2 tbsp, zucchini or summer squash: 1.5 lbs (large dice), kosher salt, freshly ground black pepper
  4. 4
    Reduce the heat to medium. Add the remaining olive oil and yellow onion , season with kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper , and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and just beginning to brown, ⏱️ 6-8 minutes .
    olive oil: 1 tbsp, yellow onion: 1 (diced), kosher salt, freshly ground black pepper
  5. 5
    Add garlic , fresh thyme , and bay leaf and cook until fragrant, about ⏱️ 30 seconds .
    garlic: 2 cloves (minced), fresh thyme: 2 sprigs, bay leaf: 1
  6. 6
    Add tomatoes and bell pepper . Add the reserved eggplant and zucchini and gently stir to combine.
    tomatoes: 1 lbs (large dice), bell pepper: 1 large (dice)
  7. 7
    Bring to a simmer, then turn down the heat to medium-low. Simmer, stirring occasionally, for at least ⏱️ 20 minutes or up to ⏱️ 90 minutes . A shorter cooking time will leave the vegetables in larger, more distinct pieces; longer cooking times will break the vegetables down into a silky stew.
  8. 8
    Remove the bay leaf and fresh thyme .
    bay leaf, fresh thyme
  9. 9
    Just before serving, stir in basil leaves . Taste and season with kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper as needed. Serve, sprinkling each serving with more basil leaves and drizzling with more olive oil .
    basil leaves: 1/4 cup (thinly sliced), kosher salt, freshly ground black pepper, basil leaves, olive oil
  10. 📝

    Making a larger batch: This recipe can be doubled and adapted to use whatever vegetables you have. Flavor extras: For something different, try adding a tablespoon of smoked paprika, a pinch of red pepper flakes, 1/4 cup of red wine, or a splash of vinegar to the ratatouille. Storage: Leftovers can be refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 5 days.
